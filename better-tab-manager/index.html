Better Tab Manager

Store and restore your tabs in one click. Turn a window full of tabs into one tidy list, then bring back a single tab or the whole list whenever you need it.

Add to Chrome View the source →

Works in Chrome and Brave. No account needed.

What else it does

  • Name, tag, color and pin your lists
  • Drag tabs and lists to reorder them, several at a time
  • Search across every tab you've stored
  • Recently deleted, so a misclick is never final
  • Remove duplicate tabs within a list
  • Copy any list as Markdown
  • Export and import your lists as JSON
  • Light and dark themes, and a background of your own
  • In English, Spanish, French, Russian and Chinese
Privacy

Your tabs stay yours

Your lists live in your browser. There are no analytics, no tracking, no ads and no server of ours — the developer never sees your data. Sync is off until you turn it on, and then it only goes to places you control.

A modern rebuild
