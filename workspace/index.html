Questions

Good questions.

Is it really free?

Yes. Workspace is in beta and free, and the version you download stays free to use.

Which agents does it work with?

Any agent that reads and writes files on your Mac, like Claude Code and Codex. Workspace teaches them how it works, so they can make pages and databases for you.

Where are my notes kept?

In a folder on your Mac, as Markdown files and SQLite databases. Nothing is uploaded, there's no account, and you can open your files with any other app.

Can I bring my notes from Obsidian or Notion?

Importing from Obsidian, Notion and Airtable is next on the roadmap.

Does it work on Windows, an Intel Mac or my phone?

Not yet. It needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer). An iOS app is coming soon, and Windows and Android after that.

Who makes it?

Me, Brian. I'm building Workspace in NYC, mostly with a team of AI agents working in Workspace itself. I'd love to hear what you think: brian@brianhirsh.com.
