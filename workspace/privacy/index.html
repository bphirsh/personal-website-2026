Workspace

Privacy

Workspace keeps your files on your computer.

Your pages, databases and pictures stay in the folders you choose, as ordinary files. We don't have a copy of them, and the app doesn't send them anywhere unless you ask it to.

What Workspace sends, and when

  • Update checks. The app checks for a new version and downloads updates from our update server, hosted by Cloudflare. That request includes your computer's IP address and the app's version, as any download does.
  • Send feedback. When you send feedback, your message goes to us, along with the details shown before you send: the page you had open, what you had selected, recent keystrokes, and the app's version. Nothing is sent until you press Send.
  • Bug reports from your agents. Only if you turn this on in Settings, your AI agents can report a bug in Workspace to us. Each report is the same kind of message as Send feedback.
  • AI models, only with your key. If you add your own key for Anthropic, OpenAI or OpenRouter, the app sends the text of a job you start to that company, under your account and its privacy terms. Without a key, nothing goes to them from Workspace.

What Workspace never sends

  • No analytics, tracking or advertising identifiers.
  • Not your files, unless you send them yourself in feedback.
  • Not your secrets. Passwords and keys you keep in Workspace stay in your computer's own secure store (the Keychain on a Mac, Credential Manager on Windows).

Your feedback, kept by us

Feedback and bug reports are stored privately, read by us to improve Workspace, and never sold or shared. To have yours deleted, email us at the address below.
