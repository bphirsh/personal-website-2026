Before you install

This is an early beta, not a finished product. It may crash, behave strangely, lose data or change without warning. Please don't use it for anything you'd be upset to lose, and don't depend on it for real work.

It comes with no warranty of any kind, and I'm not liable for anything that goes wrong while you're using it. Free to use; closed source, all rights reserved.

Installing
  1. Open the .dmg and drag Workspace into Applications.
  2. Open Workspace. It's signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens normally.
