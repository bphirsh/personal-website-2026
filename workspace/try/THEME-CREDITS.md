# Where the themes' colours come from

Default, Ink and Stark are this app's own. The other five start from published palettes, each under the MIT licence, and carry our own names rather than the schemes'. Their colours were adjusted where a pair fell short of the contrast every theme must meet (`src/shared/themecontrast.ts`), and every token the palettes do not cover — hovers, selection, the sunken left bar — was drawn for this app.
