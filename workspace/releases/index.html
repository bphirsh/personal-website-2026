Release notes

What changed in each version. Workspace updates itself, so you get each one automatically.

1.2.0 Latest

11 November 2026

The biggest update yet: Workspace now teaches your AI agents how it works, and adds templates, video and audio, and a lot of database power.

🤖 Your agents

  • The Welcome helps you teach your AI agent Workspace, and checks that it learned. A Finish setting up page in the left bar shows which agents know Workspace and the ideas they left for you.
  • Workspace gives your agents skills and an AGENTS.md, so they can make pages and databases for you from any folder. A database an agent makes for you shows up by itself, with Open and Undo.

📋 Templates

  • Start a new page or database page from a template, and save any page as one.
  • Save a database's sheets, fields and views as a template, and start new databases from it.

💪 Databases

  • Lookup and rollup columns show, count or total values from linked rows.
  • Date filters can use relative dates, like today, next week or the past 30 days.
  • A Medium row height, with a default you can set for every view.
  • Board cards show under your pointer as you drag them, and you can reorder them within a column.
  • Quicker editing: a new column is ready to name as you type, an empty date starts on today, and Shift-Enter adds a row right where you are.

🦋 Pages

  • Math written in $ signs is drawn as typeset math.
  • A page's properties are a tidy form, so editing them can't break the page.
  • Lists can be lettered (a, b, c) or use roman numerals (i, ii, iii).
  • Plain web addresses can be clicked, and tables keep long text readable, with columns you can drag wider.
  • A new Links view shows the pages linking to a page and the pages it links to.
  • Code pages have a Format Document button, including for SQL and TOML.

🎬 Video and audio

  • Videos and audio files play in a tab, and start when opened (there's a setting to turn that off).
  • More Mac formats play, including Apple Lossless, AIFF and ProRes.
  • Arrow keys move between the pictures, videos and sounds in a folder.

🗂️ Tabs and windows

  • Pin tabs: a pinned tab stays on the left as just its icon.
  • When tabs don't fit, a button at the end of the tab bar lists them all.
  • Long pages show a thin scroll bar.
  • Aliases and shortcuts show in the left bar and open what they point to.

💻 On your Mac

  • A large vault opens at once, without waiting for its whole folder tree.
  • Open web page files (.html) from Finder, and Open in Terminal from any folder.
  • A tidier menu bar and Settings, and a friendlier installer.
  • Buttons on notices, like Undo, work again, and if one page fails to draw, the rest of the window keeps working.

1.1.5

10 November 2026
  • You can drag the window by the top of the left bar again, even when the tab bar is scrolled. Before, tabs scrolled out of view got in the way.
  • No more false crash reports when Workspace can't reach the update server, for example when you're offline. Before, each failed check left a crash report for the next launch to offer.
  • Control-Command-F enters and leaves full screen, and the View menu says Exit Full Screen while you're in it.
  • Prompt text in Agents lines up with its arrow.

1.1.4

9 November 2026
  • A web link in a PDF, or in a web page you keep in your workspace, now opens in your browser. Before, it could load the site inside the Workspace window with no way back.
  • The update notice is now a single Restart to update button, so it's clear the app restarts.
  • A database row's side panel shows the built-in columns (ID, created, last updated) above the others, and the ID has a small copy button.
  • Prompts in the Agents area start collapsed to their titles. Press one to open it; Copy still takes the whole prompt.
  • A Mermaid diagram can be expanded to fill its pane. Escape takes you back to the page.
  • In Agents, Settings and Changes, Escape on an open menu closes just the menu; the next Escape leaves the page.
