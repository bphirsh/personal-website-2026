Release notes

What changed in each version. Workspace updates itself, so you get each one automatically.

1.3.3 Latest

25 November 2026

Small fixes, most of them about keeping what you wrote.

🛟 Your work, kept

  • Words you're still typing in a database cell, a row or a name are kept when you quit or close the window.
  • A picture still shown on a page stays out of the Trash when you indent a list or type a backtick.
  • A page in Deleted rows is kept for 30 days from when it was deleted, not from its last edit.
  • A prompt Workspace can't read says so, and its file is never saved over.

💪 Databases

  • In a sorted or grouped view, a row you're editing stays where it is until you press Escape or move to another row.
  • The All view is always there and can't be deleted.
  • Clicking the database in a row page's breadcrumb opens a view that shows the row, with the row selected.
  • Undoing a change to a column's type brings back its choices, number format and date format, and you can redo it.

🦋 Pages

  • Renaming a property, editing a table in a quote, changing a table's columns and marking part of a link change only what you meant.

📥 Import

  • Importing keeps every page, every row of a file like Contacts (1).csv, names with a year in them, and properties in any language.

🔍 Search

  • Go to File finds a path or name pasted with backticks, quotes or angle brackets around it.
  • Pasting a very long run of letters, such as a token, into Search no longer closes the app.
  • A folder opened in Workspace from another app shows its folder page.

🤖 Agents

  • Workspace always keeps its short note for agents in AGENTS.md and CLAUDE.md, so the setting for it is gone. A folder where it was turned off gets the note the next time it opens.

🪟 Windows

  • Undoing a delete from the left bar brings the page back instead of undoing typing in the open page.
  • Settings › Scrollbar applies to every scrollbar.
  • Claude Code is found where its installer puts it, even outside PATH.

1.3.2

22 November 2026

Workspace comes to Windows. It runs on Windows 11, signed, installs just for you with no administrator needed, and updates itself like the Mac app does. Download it.

🪟 Windows, the Windows way

  • Windows' own keys: Ctrl instead of ⌘, F11 for full screen, Ctrl+Y to redo, Alt+Left and Alt+Right for Back and Forward, Ctrl+Tab between tabs, Ctrl+W to close a tab, and F2 to rename.
  • The window has Windows' own minimise, maximise and close buttons in your theme's colours, the left bar sits on Mica, and the menu opens with Alt or the button at the left of the tab bar.
  • Windows' own words: File Explorer, the Recycle Bin, this PC, and Preview for Quick Look. Delete moves a file to the Recycle Bin, with Undo.
  • Open a file from File Explorer and it opens in Workspace, in the window already running if there is one.
  • Open in Browser lets Windows pick your app for web pages, and Open in Terminal, VS Code, Cursor, Claude Code or Codex all work, with the terminal opening in Windows Terminal.
  • Your agents get sqlite3 with Workspace, and every database tells them where it is and how to use it.
  • Uninstalling gives .md and .html files back to the app that opened them before.
  • Not shown on Windows yet: HEIC pictures and Apple Lossless, AIFF and ProRes files. They say so instead of looking broken.

💻 On the Mac too

  • Claude in Terminal and iTerm is offered only when Claude Code is installed, instead of opening a terminal that says the command was not found.

1.3.1

22 November 2026

Your agents get their own command for Workspace, spreadsheets open in a viewer, and pasting from other apps comes in clean.

🤖 Agents

  • Your AI agents get a workspace command, with help that teaches it: they can read and change your pages and databases, search, and see what changed.
  • Once you allow it, your agents can report a bug in Workspace for you.
  • Roles, templates, prompts and skills now live in one place in your home folder.

🦋 Pages

  • Pasting from Notion, Google Docs, Word or Apple Notes comes in clean.
  • Links and pictures follow a file when it moves.
  • A page you have open no longer overwrites a change an agent just made.

💪 Databases

  • Spreadsheet files (.csv, .tsv, .xlsx, .ods and .numbers) open in a viewer.
  • A Link column, breadcrumbs on a row's page, and ⌘A to select cells.
  • A view keeps its place when you switch away and come back.
  • Databases in a folder synced with Syncthing, iCloud Drive or Dropbox can be edited on several devices. If copies conflict, Workspace gathers them into one question before anything goes to the Trash.

🗂️ Folders and tabs

  • Links reach across every folder you add, and new folders and databases are ready at once.
  • Quick View moves with the arrow keys, and Collapse all closes the left bar.
  • Right-click a tab to split it or move it, and new tabs open beside the page they came from.
  • New Window from the Dock.

⚙️ Settings

  • What's new shows each version's notes.
  • Search in Settings finds a setting along with the options under it.

1.3.0

16 November 2026

Bring your data in, and find anything: Workspace now imports from Notion, Airtable, Obsidian, CSV and Excel, and has a new search.

📥 Import

  • Import… in the File menu takes a CSV or Excel file, an Airtable or Notion export, or a whole Obsidian vault. A preview shows what will be made before anything is written: change a column's type, leave things out, then Import, with Undo after.
  • An Obsidian vault comes in as its folders and databases, with its links kept.
  • Or hand any export to your own agent, which goes through it with you in chat and brings it in, pictures and databases included.

🔍 Search

  • A new search window: recent pages when nothing is typed, any file from part of its name or with a typo, and a preview of the result with your words marked.
  • Narrow it by folder, by kind, or by when something was last edited.
  • Paste a file's full path into Go to File (⌘O) to open it.

🗂️ Like Finder

  • Drag a file or folder out to Finder, Mail or any app, and it arrives as the file itself.
  • Press Space for Quick Look, and Quick Look in Finder now shows pages and databases the way Workspace does.
  • On a folder page, click to select, double-click to open, and drag to select several.

💪 Databases

  • Select several cells and type, paste or delete to change them all at once.
  • Board columns collapse to a narrow strip, and show as you drag them.
  • Filter on when rows were created or last changed.
  • Databases open faster, and sheets are now called collections.

🦋 Pages

  • Tables in pages: select cells, copy them out as a real table, paste blocks in, sort by a column, and ⌘Z without clicking out first.
  • A folder with no README shows a text area: click and start writing.
  • Enter at the end of a link keeps the link.

💻 On your Mac

  • Hold ⌘Q (or press it twice) to quit, so a stray press doesn't.
  • ⌘Z brings back a file, folder or database you just deleted.
  • The search index takes about half the disk space. It rebuilds once, the first time you open 1.3.0.

1.2.0

11 November 2026

The biggest update yet: Workspace now teaches your AI agents how it works, and adds templates, video and audio, and a lot of database power.

🤖 Your agents

  • The Welcome helps you teach your AI agent Workspace, and checks that it learned. A Finish setting up page in the left bar shows which agents know Workspace and the ideas they left for you.
  • Workspace gives your agents skills and an AGENTS.md, so they can make pages and databases for you from any folder. A database an agent makes for you shows up by itself, with Open and Undo.

📋 Templates

  • Start a new page or database page from a template, and save any page as one.
  • Save a database's sheets, fields and views as a template, and start new databases from it.

💪 Databases

  • Lookup and rollup columns show, count or total values from linked rows.
  • Date filters can use relative dates, like today, next week or the past 30 days.
  • A Medium row height, with a default you can set for every view.
  • Board cards show under your pointer as you drag them, and you can reorder them within a column.
  • Quicker editing: a new column is ready to name as you type, an empty date starts on today, and Shift-Enter adds a row right where you are.

🦋 Pages

  • Math written in $ signs is drawn as typeset math.
  • A page's properties are a tidy form, so editing them can't break the page.
  • Lists can be lettered (a, b, c) or use roman numerals (i, ii, iii).
  • Plain web addresses can be clicked, and tables keep long text readable, with columns you can drag wider.
  • A new Links view shows the pages linking to a page and the pages it links to.
  • Code pages have a Format Document button, including for SQL and TOML.

🎬 Video and audio

  • Videos and audio files play in a tab, and start when opened (there's a setting to turn that off).
  • More Mac formats play, including Apple Lossless, AIFF and ProRes.
  • Arrow keys move between the pictures, videos and sounds in a folder.

🗂️ Tabs and windows

  • Pin tabs: a pinned tab stays on the left as just its icon.
  • When tabs don't fit, a button at the end of the tab bar lists them all.
  • Long pages show a thin scroll bar.
  • Aliases and shortcuts show in the left bar and open what they point to.

💻 On your Mac

  • A large vault opens at once, without waiting for its whole folder tree.
  • Open web page files (.html) from Finder, and Open in Terminal from any folder.
  • A tidier menu bar and Settings, and a friendlier installer.
  • Buttons on notices, like Undo, work again, and if one page fails to draw, the rest of the window keeps working.

1.1.5

10 November 2026
  • You can drag the window by the top of the left bar again, even when the tab bar is scrolled. Before, tabs scrolled out of view got in the way.
  • No more false crash reports when Workspace can't reach the update server, for example when you're offline. Before, each failed check left a crash report for the next launch to offer.
  • Control-Command-F enters and leaves full screen, and the View menu says Exit Full Screen while you're in it.
  • Prompt text in Agents lines up with its arrow.

1.1.4

9 November 2026
  • A web link in a PDF, or in a web page you keep in your workspace, now opens in your browser. Before, it could load the site inside the Workspace window with no way back.
  • The update notice is now a single Restart to update button, so it's clear the app restarts.
  • A database row's side panel shows the built-in columns (ID, created, last updated) above the others, and the ID has a small copy button.
  • Prompts in the Agents area start collapsed to their titles. Press one to open it; Copy still takes the whole prompt.
  • A Mermaid diagram can be expanded to fill its pane. Escape takes you back to the page.
  • In Agents, Settings and Changes, Escape on an open menu closes just the menu; the next Escape leaves the page.
